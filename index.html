Frequently asked questions

QUESTIONS? WE'VE GOT ANSWERS.

Helpful details about the AnatoMap Pro experience.

Do I need prior experience in massage therapy or manual therapy?

No. AnatoMap Pro is organized to support anatomy review for students and interested professionals. It is an educational reference, not a clinical certification or a replacement for training.

Is AnatoMap Pro suitable for physical therapy students?

Yes. The regional anatomy organization, visual maps, and study-oriented features are intended to be useful alongside formal coursework.

Is this a PDF bundle or an app?

The main product is a browser-based member app with visual anatomy content. The three companion bonuses may include printable digital resources.

Will I need to install anything?

The product is designed to work in a modern web browser on phones, tablets, and computers. You do not need to download a native app from an app store.

What's the difference between Essential and Deluxe?

Essential includes 30 selected maps and region browsing. Deluxe adds the complete 100+ map library, extended reference content, and the full study tools described above.

Can I print the materials?

The bonus posters and worksheets are intended for printing where the delivered file and license permit it. The primary app content is designed for on-screen exploration.

Does a referred pain map tell me why someone hurts?

No. Referred pain and trigger point illustrations are educational descriptions, not patient-specific diagnostic tools. Pain can have many causes and requires appropriate clinical evaluation.

Can I use this instead of professional clinical training?

No. The app is an educational reference and does not replace an accredited course, professional licensure, clinical judgment, or medical advice.

How do I purchase and access my account?

Choose Essential or Deluxe and select its purchase button to open the corresponding Hotmart checkout. Follow the access instructions provided after purchase.
